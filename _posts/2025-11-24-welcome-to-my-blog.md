I'm excited to launch this new blog space! After years of using Twitter, I wanted a platform where I could share my thoughts, research updates, and insights about machine learning, automotive technology, and formal verification without the constraints of a 280-character limit.

## What to Expect

This blog will be a place where I share:

- **Deep dives into AI/ML topics** - Especially around formal verification of neural networks
- **Company updates from roboGaze** - Our journey in automotive ML
- **Technical articles** - Code, algorithms, and best practices
- **Personal reflections** - On technology, science, and innovation

## Why Leave Twitter?

While Twitter was great for quick updates and networking, I felt the need for a more controlled, personal space where I can:

1. Share longer-form content
2. Include detailed images and diagrams
3. Maintain a permanent archive of my thoughts
4. Own my content completely

## What's Next?

I'll be posting regularly about:

- The latest developments in formal verification
- Automotive AI safety standards (ASPICE, ASIL)
- Conference talks and presentations
- Interesting papers and research

Stay tuned for more content! You can still find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tgergo1/) and [GitHub](https://github.com/tgergo1/) for professional updates.
