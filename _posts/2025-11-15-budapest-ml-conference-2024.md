Last week, I had the privilege of presenting at the Budapest ML Conference 2024. The energy and innovation in the Hungarian ML community continue to impress me!

## My Talk: Formal Verification of Neural Networks

I presented our work on formal verification techniques for neural networks in automotive applications. The main points covered:

- Current challenges in ML verification
- Mathematical foundations of formal methods
- Practical applications in autonomous vehicles
- Integration with ASPICE/ASIL workflows

The audience was highly engaged, with excellent questions about scalability and real-world deployment challenges.

## Other Highlights

**Synthetic Data Generation Panel**
The panel on synthetic data was fascinating. As someone working with data collection for automotive ML, I found the discussions about:
- Quality vs. quantity trade-offs
- Domain gap challenges
- Validation strategies

**Networking**

Connected with amazing researchers and practitioners from:
- BME (Budapest University of Technology)
- Local startups
- International companies with Hungarian offices

## Reflections

The Budapest ML scene is thriving. It's exciting to see so much talent and innovation coming from Hungary. Our work at roboGaze is part of this broader ecosystem of excellence.

Looking forward to next year's conference!
